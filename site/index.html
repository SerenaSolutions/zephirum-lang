Three planes, one language

Classical

Production runtime, ~3.4k decisions/s, deterministic by seed, offline-verifiable SHA3-256 receipts, thread-safe.

Quantum

Poliglota core validated in 6 languages (Qiskit, OpenQASM 3, Cirq, PennyLane, Braket, Q#) and on real IBM Heron silicon — GHZ device-trust gate before every battery.

Post-quantum

ML-DSA-44 signatures with embedded public keys, quantum-measured entropy for receipt IDs, and Z-KEM: ML-KEM-768 + AES-256-GCM envelopes — who lacks the key reads nothing.

Frontiers under test (Rodada F, live)

CHSH non-locality gate (the device must violate Bell's inequality, S > 2.20), a 3-qubit repetition error-correcting code (logical error vs physical error, PROTEGIDO verdict) and Grover scaled to n=4 — three certificates no toy prototype passes. Receipts chain round by round: each seal carries the previous one.
