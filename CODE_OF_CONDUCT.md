# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community. This repository applies one house rule above all: no claims above evidence — technical honesty is part of our code of conduct.

## Our Standards
Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include: demonstrating empathy and kindness; respecting different opinions and disagreements; giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback; publishing results with their receipts.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include: harassment or discrimination in any form; publishing others' private information without consent; overstating experimental results or presenting simulations as hardware executions; other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement
Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the repository owner via GitHub's Report Content mechanism or by opening a private security advisory when integrity is involved. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
