# Support

How to get help with ZEPHIRUM / ZYQL:

- **Questions & discussion** — [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/SerenaSolutions/zephirum-lang/discussions) (Q&A and Ideas categories). We answer, including "your example output is wrong".
- **Bugs and evidence problems** — [open an issue](https://github.com/SerenaSolutions/zephirum-lang/issues/new/choose). Reports that break a receipt's integrity are treated as security-adjacent.
- **Security disclosures** — see SECURITY.md (private vulnerability reporting).
- **Learning path** — docs/LEARN.md, docs/COOKBOOK.md, docs/MIGRATION.md (every output recorded from real runs).

Response expectation: we are a small operation; discussions get answers within days, not hours. Nothing passes silently.
